About The Gearhounds

We are Eaton Rapids Robotics, also known as The Gearhounds, a dedicated high school robotics team committed to inspiring students to pursue careers in STEM fields through hands-on robotics education and competition.

Our team combines technical innovation with creative problem-solving, preparing students for future success in engineering, programming, and technology. Through our participation in robotics competitions, we build not just robots, but also leadership skills, teamwork, and a passion for learning.
